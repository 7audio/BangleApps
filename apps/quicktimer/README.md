# Quick Timer

A simple touch timer with 1-, 2-, and 3-minute presets, plus a “nxt” shortcut
that starts a 2-minute timer.

Tap a duration to start the countdown. The most recently started duration is
shown at the top of the screen and is saved between launches. When the timer
ends, the watch vibrates with a short-short-long pattern. The selected duration
is shown beside the countdown.

Press the hardware button to cycle through the duration pages and a third page
that lists the timers that ran to completion most recently (e.g.
`1,2,|,5,10,21,3`), most recent last. The “nxt” shortcut appears as `|` in
history.
