# Quick Timer

A simple touch timer with four preset durations: 1, 2, 3, and 4 minutes.

Tap a duration to start the countdown. The most recently started duration is
shown at the top of the screen and is saved between launches. When the timer
ends, the watch vibrates with a short-short-long pattern.

Press the hardware button to cycle through the duration pages and a third page
that lists the timers you ran most recently (e.g. `1,2,5,10,21,3`), most recent last.
